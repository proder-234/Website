Privacy Policy for FitCheck

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

FitCheck ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the FitCheck iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide while using the app, including:

  • Profile Information: Name, email address, age, height, weight, gender, and health goals.
  • Meal & Food Data: Foods you log, nutritional estimates, meal photos you choose to analyze, and timestamps.
  • Text Input: Food descriptions, portion sizes, or notes you enter.
  • Health Data (Optional): When you connect Apple Health (HealthKit), we may access activity and nutrition data you authorize.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide calorie and nutrition estimates
  • Generate personalized health insights and recommendations
  • Track progress toward your health goals
  • Sync dietary data with Apple Health when enabled
  • Improve app performance and user experience

3. AI Processing and Cloud Services

FitCheck uses Google’s Gemini AI service to analyze meal photos and text descriptions.

  • When you use food photo analysis, the selected image and related text are securely sent to Google’s servers for processing.
  • Before sending images, the app performs on-device face detection. If a face is detected, the image is not sent to the cloud.
  • Only the specific content needed for analysis is sent — your profile data, meal history, and HealthKit data are not shared with Google.
  • Google processes data according to its own privacy policies and terms. We do not control Google’s data retention practices.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, FitCheck may read and write the following HealthKit data:

  • Read: Dietary energy, active energy burned, step count, heart rate, and walking/running distance
  • Write: Dietary calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, and saturated fat from meals you log

HealthKit data is used only within the app and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

5. Data Storage

Your profile data, meal logs, and food history are stored locally on your device. FitCheck does not maintain a central user database for this information.

Images and text are only sent to cloud services when you actively use AI analysis features.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared:

  • With Google’s AI service when you request food analysis
  • With Apple Health when you enable HealthKit integration
  • If required by law

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Delete your meal history and profile data inside the app
  • Disconnect HealthKit at any time in Apple Health settings
  • Stop using AI features at any time

Deleting your in-app data does not remove data previously processed by Google. Please refer to Google’s policies for more details.

9. Security

We use device-level security, Apple’s secure frameworks, and encrypted network connections to protect your data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date.
