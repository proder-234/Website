Privacy Policy for Mausam Nepal

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

Introduction

Mausam Nepal is a weather information application that provides real-time weather data and forecasts for locations within Nepal. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Data Sources

Mausam Nepal collects and displays weather information from the following sources:

  • Nepal Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) - Primary data source
  • Open-Meteo - Supplementary weather data service

Language and Content

  • Supports English and Nepali languages
  • Some features only available in Nepali due to direct government data extraction
  • Data displayed as provided by sources

Location Services

  • Works exclusively for locations within Nepal
  • Outside Nepal? Must select a Nepali location
  • Location data only used to show relevant weather, not stored or shared

Information We Collect

Mausam Nepal does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We only:

  • Access your location (with your permission)
  • Display publicly available weather data

Features Provided

  • Current weather conditions
  • 3-hour forecast
  • 3-day forecast
  • Major cities forecast
  • Latest bulletins: Cold wave monitoring, Monsoon monitoring, High altitude forecast, 24-hour and 3-day forecast bulletins, Weekly outlook

Weather Stations and Their Features

Mausam Nepal uses weather station data from the Nepal Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM). Station-related features include:

  • Station data source: Observations from DHM automatic weather stations across Nepal (via official DHM API)
  • Nearby stations: Shows the nearest station and regional comparison (next nearest stations) based on your or selected location
  • Station explorer: Map and list of all DHM stations; browse by location and see station names, coordinates, and current observations (e.g. temperature)
  • Location use: Your device location (or chosen location) is used only to rank and show nearest stations; not stored or shared with third parties
  • Caching: Station lists and nearby results may be cached temporarily on your device for faster loading; no personal data is stored

Visual Experience

  • App themes automatically change based on weather conditions
  • Done locally on your device, no data collected

Third-Party Services

We use Open-Meteo API to supplement weather data. Please refer to their Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal data at risk. All weather data displayed is publicly available information from government sources.

Disclaimer

Mausam Nepal is a non-commercial, independent application created solely to make publicly available weather information more accessible to the people of Nepal.

This app:

  • Does not monetize or profit from weather data
  • Simply aggregates and displays information already published by government sources
  • Has no commercial intent or advertising
  • Is developed as a public service for easier access to official weather information

All weather data displayed is the same information provided by the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) and other official sources, presented in a user-friendly mobile format.
