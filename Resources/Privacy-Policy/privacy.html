BloomCycle Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 3, 2025

Information We Collect

BloomCycle collects only the information you choose to enter, including menstrual cycle length, period duration, last period start date, and optional notes.

How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide cycle tracking and educational features within the app.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Data Sharing

BloomCycle does not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third parties.

Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data:

• You may edit or delete individual entries within the app.

• You can clear all data at once using the button "Clear All Data".

• Deleting the app from your device will permanently remove all stored data.

• BloomCycle does not retain any copies of your data after deletion.

Medical Disclaimer

BloomCycle is for educational purposes only and is not a medical device or a source of medical advice.

Internet Usage

BloomCycle can be used fully offline.

Some educational content includes links to Youtube videos. These links are user-initiated and open outside the app. BloomCycle does not control or collect data from external websites.
