Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 3, 2026

By using Balanzy, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

What We Collect

Balanzy collects the following information to provide expense-splitting services:

  • Account Information: Email, display name, Apple ID or Google account (if using third-party sign-in)
  • Expense Data: Expense details, group information, split arrangements, balances
  • Technical Data: Device information, app usage, multipeer connectivity and local network data for sharing features

How We Use Your Data

  • Manage your account and authenticate sign-ins
  • Track shared expenses and calculate balances
  • Enable group collaboration and expense splitting
  • Send notifications about expenses and payments

Data Sharing

Balanzy does not sell your personal information.

Your data is shared only with:

  • Group members
  • Firebase/Google Cloud Platform (secure hosting and authentication)
  • Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In (if you use these services)
  • Other users via peer-to-peer

Security & Storage

Your data is protected with:

  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore database
  • Local caching for offline access (cleared on logout)

Account deletion removes your profile and anonymizes your expenses in shared groups.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your profile anytime
  • Delete your account in Profile settings
  • Opt out of notifications in Settings

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States and other countries where our service providers, including Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform), operate. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your information in these countries.
